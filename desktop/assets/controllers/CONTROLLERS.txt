controller-gameboy.png / .svg
Original illustration created for ROM-in-a-Box. No third-party artwork.

Runtime PNGs are 1920x760 (2x of the 960x380 menu scene), rasterised from the
SVG with librsvg. Regenerate with:
  rsvg-convert -w 1920 -h 760 -o controller-<name>.png controller-<name>.svg
This is a developer asset operation; exported applications do not require it.

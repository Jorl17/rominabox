Original ROM-in-a-Box menu cues, synthesised by scripts/native_runtime/generate-menu-sounds.mjs
from scripts/native_runtime/menu-sound-synthesis.mjs. No game recordings and no third-party audio.
Console voices are modelled from published hardware behaviour, not sampled. 44100 Hz, 16-bit, mono.

One pack is one complete set of up, down, ok and cancel. Movement cues are mixed about 10 dB under
the confirm cues because the menu plays one on every focus change and pointer hover.

blip      Blip      dmg-blip + soft scroll — Plain 4-bit handheld pulse. The unadorned menu tick.
arpeggio  Arpeggio  dmg-arp + soft scroll — Quick note runs on confirm. The busiest, most chiptune set.
square    Square    md-psg + soft scroll — Thin, bright square-wave chip. Arcade cabinet, not FM.
arcade    Arcade    md-arcade + own scroll — Rising coin-op confirm with quick sweeps on move.
bell      Bell      md-bell + soft scroll — Bright, musical FM bell. Clean and tuneful.
clang     Clang     md-clang + soft scroll — Metallic, inharmonic FM. Sharp and aggressive.
thud      Thud      md-thud + soft scroll — Low FM body under a bright tick. Chunky and bass-forward.
warm      Warm      md-warm + own scroll — Mellow FM at a low index. Rounded, with no hard edge.

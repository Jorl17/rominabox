These presets are original to ROM-in-a-Box.

They are GLSL in the form RetroArch's OpenGL driver compiles: one file
split by VERTEX and FRAGMENT, with the uniform names that driver defines.
They are not taken from the libretro shader repositories. Those packs were
not reviewed for redistribution, and this build does not ship them.

Exported games run the OpenGL driver. Metal and Vulkan are switched off at
build time, and slang presets need one of those (or GLCore). A preset in
another language is refused rather than turning those drivers back on.
